Card AD-143: time on cards and a board Stats strip.

## What changed

- **Core** (`crates/core/src/timing.rs`, new, pure): `card_times(task, history, now)` folds a card's history lines (created, pulled, moved, archived, unarchived) with the card's own `created_at`, `lane_since` and `archived_at` into its stays lane by lane (`Stay {lane, from, to?}`), each lane's total (`LaneTime {lane, seconds, stays, current}`), its first pull and when it reached Done. `Stats::of(board, cards)` gives a range's numbers: cards done, median pull to Done, median finished time in Review, and each holder's own. Unit tests cover a card that moved back and forth, one never pulled, one still in its lane now, a renewing pull, lines out of order, a history with gaps, time off the board, the medians and the wire form.
- **Protocol**: `tasks` answers `{tasks, more, times: CardTimes[]}` (one per card on the page; `task_search` sends none) and `task_detail` carries `times` from the card's whole history. The daemon folds under its state lock with its own clock. ARCHITECTURE's table says the shape and the fold's rules.
- **CLI**: `task list` gets an `IN LANE` column; `task show` prints a `time` line (each lane's total, `×n` stays, `(now)`) and `pull to done`. The MCP tools `list_tasks` and `get_task` follow the response; their descriptions say so.
- **App**: a card's face says how long its lane has had it in words beside the due chip (*2h 10m in lane*); the open card lists its stays lane by lane (when each began, how long, *so far* for the current one) and *Pull to done*; under the board toolbar a Stats strip gives the range on view (the cards shown after the search and filters) as labelled numbers: *Done*, *Pull to done*, *In Review*, then each holder's done count and median. Nothing hover-only, no extra screen. DESKTOP-UX and GUIDE updated.

## How to try it

- `agentdocker task list` and `agentdocker task show <key>` against a board with moved cards.
- `get_task` / `list_tasks` from an MCP session: `times` on each.
- In the app, open a project's Board: the face words, an open card's timeline, and the strip once a card on view is done or has left Review; narrow with the search or a filter and the numbers follow.

## What it will not do

- No new screen and no chart: one strip of numbers, narrowed by the filters already there.
- A card from before the history was kept has one stay, from its `lane_since`; the fold does not guess earlier lanes.
- Time off the board (archived) is counted to no lane.
- The median in Review counts finished stays only, so a card sitting in Review now does not move it.

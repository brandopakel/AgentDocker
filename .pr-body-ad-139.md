## What changed

**More → Usage** gains a chart of the window between its four totals and its table.

- Over 24 hours it is one bar an hour; over 7 or 30 days one bar a day (UTC days, as the hour rows are). Each bar stacks input, cache read, cache write and output, told apart by pattern (solid, diagonal, dotted, lined) and the legend's words, never by hue alone.
- A bar's tooltip, and its value to a screen reader (`usage-bar-<n>`, a `Label` node named by its span), is its span, each count with its coverage mark (`~`, `—`) and its samples.
- An hour or day nothing was reported for is a gap with no bar, said as *Nothing reported*; no zero is invented. The hour or day the clock is in has a dashed edge and says *Still filling*.
- The legend ends with the tallest bar's tokens, the chart's scale.
- Drawn with Iced's canvas (tiny-skia geometry, one cached canvas a bucket), so it works with the software renderer. The legend's swatches are the same canvas at fourteen points.

The folding is pure and in core: `agentdocker_core::usage::chart` turns a report by hour into buckets of an hour or a day over its range, every bucket present, `None` where no row fell in it, the bucket holding the report's clock marked filling, with coverage merged the way the totals merge it (complete only where every hour was). It refuses what it cannot place (a report by anything but hour, a row without an hour or outside the range, more than a thousand buckets) rather than guess.

When the table is read by hour the chart draws its rows; otherwise the screen makes a second read of the same window by hour, kept with its window and never drawn for another's. The CLI is unchanged.

Docs: `docs/DESKTOP-UX.md` (the screen), `docs/ICED-DESIGN.md` (the drawing, the ids), `docs/GUIDE.md` (one line under Usage).

## How to try it

With usage collection on (`[usage] enabled = true` in `agentd.toml`), open a project, **More → Usage**, and switch between **24 hours**, **7 days** and **30 days**. Hover a bar for its counts; the last bar has a dashed edge while the hour or day is still filling; hours with nothing reported are empty. Switch the grouping: the chart stays the window by hour. `scripts/iced_workflow_smoke.py` already captures the Usage screen (`usage.png`).

## What it will not do

- No y-axis: the scale is the tallest bar's tokens in the legend, and every bar's exact counts are on its tooltip.
- Reasoning tokens are not a fifth series: providers count them inside output.
- Days are UTC days, as the hour rows in the table are.
- It does not place the sources' gaps on the time line: the report counts them but does not say where they fall, so they stay in the totals' tooltips.

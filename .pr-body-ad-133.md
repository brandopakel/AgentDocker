Stacked on #513 (the macOS menu-bar item); base `claude/ad-132-menu-bar-item`. Card AD-133.

## What changed

- Clicking the menu-bar item now opens a panel under it instead of raising the window: a second Iced window of the same application (448 × 600 points, borderless, fixed, floating, focused as it opens), placed from the item's button frame (`status_item::Anchor`) and slid sideways to stay on its screen. The app runs as an `iced::daemon` so that it can open one; the main window is opened at boot with the settings it had.
- The panel lists, across every listed project: first the rows Needs you holds (asker, the ask or the errand chip, the time-left chip, and **Answer** / **Allow once** / **Deny** / **Go to Terminal** inline, the card's own widgets sending the card's own messages, each row led by its project), then the agents at work under a line per project (name order, *Other sessions* last): mark, name, standing chip, the card it holds (the title in its `task:` lease's note) and how long it has been at it, the longest first. With nothing waiting: *Nothing waits on you*; with no project: one line pointing at the window. The foot is **Open AgentDocker**.
- A row, **Answer** and an in-app terminal close the panel and bring the window to the front at that agent (back from the menu bar when it was closed there); a terminal in another app only closes it. **Allow once** / **Deny** leave it open.
- It closes on Escape, on focus loss (a click anywhere else), on the item's click again, and when the item is turned off. The click that took the focus closes it and does not reopen it (`PANEL_REOPEN_GUARD`).
- The panel reads the window's own snapshot: no second subscription, no second connection, no request to open it. While it is open the clock sweep runs at the focused pace so its time-left chips stay true.
- Window events now carry their window (`Message::WindowEvent`, from `listen_with`'s window id): the panel's are its own, the main window's are handled as before.
- Needs you's rows are factored out (`needs_you_rows(everywhere, narrow)`); the card is unchanged.
- Smoke harness: a `menu_bar_panel` step sends the item's click and `capture` takes `panel: true` to screenshot the newest window; the workflow smoke opens, captures and closes the panel.
- Docs: `docs/DESKTOP-UX.md` has the panel's contract; `docs/ICED-DESIGN.md` its interaction rules and the smoke steps.

## How to try it

Build and run the app on macOS with the menu bar item on (the default). Click the item: the panel opens under it with what waits and who is working; press Escape or click elsewhere and it closes. With a question waiting, **Allow once** answers it from the panel; **Answer** or an agent's row opens the window there. Close the window with the item on, click the item, then a row or **Open AgentDocker**: the window comes back.

## What it will not do

- No tiles, counts or filters at the top (MB-3), no global shortcut (MB-4), no notification changes (MB-5).
- The panel has no native accessibility adapter of its own (the main window keeps its adapter); its rows carry the same spoken sentences as the card's.
- Linux and Windows are unchanged: no item, no panel; the daemon switch keeps the one window and the app's own exit.
- The smoke's panel capture is best-effort: the step screenshots the newest window, so if the panel lost focus and closed before the capture, the capture is the main window again rather than a failure.
